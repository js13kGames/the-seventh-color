Light used to have seven colours. The last one lived in a unicorn's horn until Darkness
broke it, and the world has been white and cold ever since. Jack goes to get it back.

The Seventh Color is a fairy tale you play through: nineteen beats, each one a scene
rather than a level. A frozen lake cracks under a rule you have to work out before the
last pane. A bog of warning lights answers a guessed order with nothing but a count of
what you got right. Three watchers keep three different rhythms, and you creep forward
only on the beat where every head is down. Moonlight goes into a witch's mirrors and has
to come out on her face. And at the end you carry sunlight down through Darkness's own
dungeon, bending it floor by floor with bucklers while patrolling guards knock your
mirrors askew — and you get one shot at opening the shaft.

Nothing here is loaded. The portraits, the scenes, the snow and the eight-track score are
all generated at runtime, from code, in 13 kilobytes.

Built for the 2026 theme "rainbows and unicorns."

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to move and choose, Space or Enter to act and advance dialogue.
